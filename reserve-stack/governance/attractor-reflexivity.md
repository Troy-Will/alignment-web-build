## Safety Banner (Anti-Glamour)

This page describes a coordination effect, not destiny.

- Not prophecy  
- Not “messages from the future”  
- Not “belief manifests reality”

If this starts to feel like inevitability: stop and return to practical proxies.

---

## What “Attractor Reflexivity” Means

**Claim (bounded):**  
Recognizing a plausible attractor can change behavior; changed behavior can change outcomes and probabilities.

This is reflexivity in the ordinary sense:
- people act differently when they believe a path is likely
- those actions feed back into the world

---

## The mechanism (plain language)

**Shared model → coordination shifts → incentives shift → outcomes shift**

Examples (generic):
- If people expect conflict, they arm and conflict becomes likelier.
- If people expect stability, they cooperate and stability becomes likelier.

This is not magic. It is feedback.

---

## What this is NOT

Attractor reflexivity is not:
- “the future sent a message”
- “the universe guarantees a path”
- “saying the right words causes a response”
- “belief alone produces reality”

The only causal pathway allowed here is:
**models change behavior; behavior changes systems.**

---

## How to use this safely

Use attractor language only as:
- a planning lens
- a risk lens
- a coordination tool

Do not use it as:
- certainty theater
- identity or destiny framing
- bargaining narrative fuel

---

## Practical safety rules

### Rule 1: Translate models into behaviors
If someone says “Reserve is plausible,” the next question is:
**What behaviors and institutions would make it more stable?**

### Rule 2: Prefer proxies over narratives
Use checkable signals:
- low volatility
- governance capacity
- dignity invariants preserved
- boundary respect

### Rule 3: If it feels inevitable, downshift
Replace:
- “This is going to happen”
with:
- “What is one falsifiable claim to check this month?”
