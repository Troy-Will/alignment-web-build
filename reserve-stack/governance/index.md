## Safety Banner (Anti-Glamour)

This section exists to prevent drift, glamour loops, guru dynamics, and coercion creep as the framework evolves.  
It is governance hygiene—not prophecy, not negotiation.

---

## Purpose

A framework like the Reserve Stack will evolve. Without explicit process, it tends to:
- inflate complexity without clarity,
- drift into bargaining-shaped language,
- normalize surveillance or coercion “for safety,”
- become status-driven or guru-led.

These pages keep it auditable and reversible.
