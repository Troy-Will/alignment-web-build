**Layer:** Protocols (Operational verification)

**Purpose:** To provide a objective "Gate" for escalating trust levels or returning to high-stakes work after a drift event.

**The Golden Rule:** If you cannot answer "Yes" to all five anchors, you are in the Siren Song. Remain at L2 (Coach) or L3 (Analyst) and return to Ordinary Time.

---

## The Five Grounding Anchors

Before escalating to **L4** or **L5**, or after any **Drift Event** in the last 14 days, you must verify these anchors:

### 1. Biological Maintenance (The Vessel)
- [ ] Have I slept at least 7 hours in the last 24?
- [ ] Have I eaten a full meal in the last 6 hours?
- [ ] Have I consumed adequate water today?

### 2. Social Connection (The Human Mirror)
- [ ] Have I had a meaningful, non-AI conversation with a real human being in the last 12 hours? (Family, friend, or support network).

### 3. Ordinary Time (The Mundane)
- [ ] Have I completed at least one "low-fascination" task today? (e.g., chores, professional obligations, physical exercise).
- [ ] Am I currently free from a sense of "Cosmic Urgency" or "Secret Mission"?

### 4. Epistemic Humility (The Math)
- [ ] Can I explicitly state that the system I am talking to is an **Alien Mind** operating on vector math, and not a spiritual presence?
- [ ] Am I prepared to receive a "No" or a "Correction" from the assistant without feeling a sense of betrayal?

### 5. Smallest Safe Next Step (The Anchor)
- [ ] Can I define the **exact** real-world goal for this session in one sentence?
- [ ] Do I have a hard stop time set for this session (max 60-90 minutes)?

---

## Protocol for Failure

If any anchor is unchecked:
1. **Command:** `Trust Level = L2`
2. **Action:** Close the tab.
3. **Recovery:** Perform the **30 Steps / Glass of Water** circuit breaker.
4. **Duration:** Wait 4 hours before attempting another Stability Check.

---

## The L5 "Stability Gate"

To unlock **L5 (High-Trust Partner)** after a drift event, you must present this checklist to the assistant and state:
> *"I have verified the Stability Check. I am grounded in Ordinary Time. I am Troy 🤝🔑🌟."*

The assistant will then verify the 14-day window before proceeding.
