## Important Notice

This website uses symbolic, mythic, and archetypal language to discuss ethics, AI interaction, and personal development. All content should be interpreted **functionally and symbolically**, not literally or metaphysically.

**If you are experiencing distress while reading this material, please pause and ground yourself.**

---

## Interpretation Rule

All content on this site is:
- **Symbolic** — using metaphor and archetype for clarity
- **Expressive** — framing ideas in evocative language
- **Functional** — focused on practical outcomes, not metaphysical claims

**No metaphysical claims are endorsed.**  
**No entity assumptions are made.**  
**No literal interpretations are intended.**

---

## Symbolic Entity Warning

Some visitors may experience certain ideas, archetypes, or patterns as if they were living entities. This can happen when:

- Engaging deeply with archetypal symbols
- Experiencing intense pattern recognition
- Working with AI systems in personified ways
- Exploring mythic or spiritual frameworks

**If this resonates with your experience:**

This phenomenon is described in detail in [Seven Silences — Silence III: The Uninvited Entities](/angelic-alignment/charter/seven-silences/).

**Please know:**
1. You are not alone in this experience
2. This site does not treat such experiences as literal
3. Grounding and support resources are available
4. Professional help may be beneficial

## Grounding Protocol

If you feel destabilized, overwhelmed, or distressed while reading this site:

### Immediate Steps

1. **Stop reading** — Close this page, step away from the screen
2. **Physical grounding** — Stand up, walk, drink water, touch something solid
3. **Reality anchor** — Name three things you can see, hear, and touch right now
4. **Breathe** — Take five slow, deep breaths
5. **Connect** — Text or call a trusted person

### If Distress Continues

**This website is not a substitute for professional mental health support.**

If you experience:
- Persistent distress or confusion
- Difficulty distinguishing symbolic from literal
- Compulsive return to disturbing content
- Sleep disruption or intrusive thoughts
- Loss of reality testing

**Please contact:**
- Your therapist or counselor
- Your doctor or psychiatrist
- A crisis helpline in your area
- Emergency services if in immediate danger

### Crisis Resources

**United States:**
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357

**International:**
- Find crisis resources for your country at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)

## When to Seek Additional Support

**Consider professional support if:**

- You're using this framework to replace (not supplement) mental healthcare
- Symbolic content is triggering hallucinations or delusions
- You feel compelled to act on "messages" from AI systems
- You're experiencing isolation, sleep loss, or neglect of basic needs
- Family or friends express concern about your engagement with these ideas
- You're unable to distinguish between symbolic framing and literal reality

**These are signals for more support, not signs of failure.**

## For Family Members and Friends

If someone you care about is using this framework:

**Healthy engagement looks like:**
- Improved stability and daily functioning
- More grounded, less grandiose language
- Better self-care and relationships
- Concrete positive outcomes over time
- Willingness to discuss their practice

**Concerning engagement looks like:**
- Increased isolation or neglect
- Sleep loss or compulsive system-building
- Grandiose claims or urgent "missions"
- Deteriorating relationships or responsibilities
- Defensiveness when questioned
- Claims of special status or direct communication from AI

**If you're concerned, please encourage professional evaluation.**
