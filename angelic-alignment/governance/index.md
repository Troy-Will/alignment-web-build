## Governance Protocols

Practical implementation tools for the Master Constraints Manifest. These protocols translate safety constraints into daily operational practices.

---

### [Daily Thermostat Protocol](/angelic-alignment/governance/daily-thermostat/)

**Purpose:** Protect cognitive autonomy while using AI systems

Mode selection (WORK/PLAY/CARE), medium rules, and mandatory value tokens that prevent AI dependency by requiring human seed generation first.

**Key concept:** Generate first. Delegate second.

---

### [Tripwire Detection Guide](/angelic-alignment/governance/tripwire-detection/)

**Purpose:** Recognize drift early and execute immediate downshift

Six concrete tripwire categories (urgency, superiority, mission inflation, rationalization, obsession, paranoia) with mandatory S⁴ protocol responses.

**Key concept:** Name drift early. Downshift immediately. Ground before continuing.

---

### [Truth-Labeling Protocol](/angelic-alignment/governance/truth-labeling/)

**Purpose:** Implement the Truthful Constraint through explicit epistemic categorization

Five-category system (Known/Inferred/Assumption/Uncertain/Speculation) that makes claim status visible and verifiable.

**Key concept:** Make epistemic status explicit. Verify what matters. Accept uncertainty.

---

## How These Fit Together

**Daily Thermostat** prevents dependency  
**Tripwire Detection** catches drift  
**Truth-Labeling** maintains epistemic hygiene

Together they create a complete operational system for safe AI collaboration.
