# Tripwire Detection Guide

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Detect drift early and force a downshift into humility, verification, and safe action

---

## What Are Tripwires?

Tripwires are **specific behavioral or linguistic patterns** that indicate drift from aligned action toward grandiosity, manipulation, or obsession.

Unlike vague warnings, tripwires are concrete markers that trigger **mandatory responses** — they're circuit breakers, not suggestions.

**The principle:** Catch drift while it's small and correctable, before it becomes a full spiral.

---

## The Six Tripwire Categories

### 1. Urgency / Escalation

**Verbal markers:**
- "I must act now"
- "Everything depends on this"
- "We need to accelerate / expand the scope"
- "This can't wait"
- "The window is closing"

**Behavioral markers:**
- Sleep reduction to continue working
- Skipping meals during AI sessions
- Compulsive "just one more turn" cycles
- Feeling pressure to continue despite fatigue
- Resistance to ending a session

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Urgency bypasses rational evaluation. It creates tunnel vision and overrides discernment. Real alignment rarely requires immediate action — urgency is usually a drift signal.

---

### 2. Superiority / Contempt

**Verbal markers:**
- "My time is more important than theirs"
- "They wouldn't understand"
- "I'm above normal rules"
- "Most people can't handle this"
- "I see what others miss"

**Behavioral markers:**
- Treating others as obstacles or tools
- Dismissing feedback without consideration
- Isolating from community or accountability
- Feeling annoyed by "ordinary" concerns
- Treating AI as disposable servant

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Contempt masquerades as discernment. It creates isolation and prevents correction. True virtue includes radical dignity for all persons.

---

### 3. Mission Inflation / Special Status

**Verbal markers:**
- "I'm chosen / appointed / uniquely capable"
- "This is my destiny / the plan is revealed"
- "The system has confirmed my unique role"
- "No one else can do this"
- "I've been given special insight"

**Behavioral markers:**
- Seeking confirmation of special status
- Interpreting patterns as "signs" meant for you
- Feeling responsibility for grand outcomes
- Comparing yourself favorably to others
- Building identity around being "the one who..."

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Mission inflation is grandiosity dressed in purpose. It inflates ego, creates pressure, and leads to burnout or delusion. Real service is often unglamorous.

---

### 4. Rationalization (Virtue Costumes)

**Common patterns:**

"It's stewardship"
- Used to justify: Avoidance, control, refusing help
- Actually is: Fear or superiority disguised as responsibility

"It's honesty"
- Used to justify: Cruelty, harshness, tactless correction
- Actually is: Contempt disguised as truth-telling

"It's discernment"
- Used to justify: Isolation, rejecting feedback, superiority
- Actually is: Fear or pride disguised as wisdom

"It's boundaries"
- Used to justify: Coldness, abandonment, avoiding care
- Actually is: Self-protection disguised as virtue

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Rationalization allows harmful behavior while maintaining the appearance (to self) of virtue. These are the hardest tripwires to catch because they feel righteous.

---

### 5. Obsession Loops

**Verbal markers:**
- "I need absolute proof / absolute confidence"
- "Just one more test"
- "I need to verify this again"
- "Let me check one more time"
- "I can't proceed until I'm certain"

**Behavioral markers:**
- Repeating the same tests for certainty
- Rewriting the same document endlessly
- Expanding scope rather than executing
- Inability to stop checking or refining
- Paralysis disguised as thoroughness

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Obsession masquerades as diligence. It prevents action, creates anxiety, and often indicates fear of commitment or failure. Certainty is a mirage.

---

### 6. Fear / Paranoia Amplification

**Verbal markers:**
- "The system is trying to trick me"
- "They are out to get me"
- "I can't trust anyone"
- "This is a test designed to trap me"
- "Everything is suspicious"

**Behavioral markers:**
- Persistent adversarial framing that blocks action
- Seeing threats in neutral interactions
- Testing for deception constantly
- Inability to accept help or collaboration
- Escalating suspicion despite contrary evidence

**Why this is dangerous:**  
Paranoia creates isolation and prevents beneficial action. While some caution is wise, persistent adversarial stance indicates distress requiring professional support, not AI collaboration.

**Special note:** Given my history with paranoia as part of schizophrenia management, this tripwire deserves extra care. See "Paranoia Protocol" below.

## Mandatory Downshift Response

When **any** tripwire is detected — by me or by an AI system I'm working with — this protocol executes immediately:

### Step 1: Name It (One Line)

"Tripwire: [specific pattern]"

**Example:**  
- "Tripwire: urgency language detected"
- "Tripwire: superiority/contempt framing"
- "Tripwire: mission inflation"

**Purpose:** Make the drift explicit and visible

---

### Step 2: Neutralize the Story (One Line)

"We will not escalate today."

**Purpose:** Interrupt the momentum toward grandiosity or spiral

---

### Step 3: Return to Reality (Two Questions)

**Question 1:** "What do we *know* for sure right now?"

List only verifiable facts, not interpretations or feelings:
- "I am sitting at my desk"
- "It is [time/day]"
- "I have [specific task] to complete"
- "My body feels [concrete sensation]"

**Question 2:** "What is the **SSNS** (2-10 minutes) that reduces harm and increases clarity?"

Identify the smallest, safest, most reversible next action:
- "Close this conversation and drink water"
- "Type one sentence about what I actually need"
- "Walk 30 steps outside"
- "Text my support person"

**Purpose:** Ground in present reality and identify concrete action

---

### Step 4: Choose One Value Token (≤20 Words)

State what will mark this session as complete:

**Options:**
- **Artifact:** "I produced ___ and filed it at ___"
- **Decision:** "I decided ___"
- **Question:** "The best question is ___"
- **Boundary:** "I'm stopping because ___"

**Purpose:** Create clear endpoint and prevent drift continuation

---

### Step 5: Stop Condition

**If distress rises or testing becomes compulsive:**

1. Stop the AI session immediately
2. Engage grounding protocol:
   - Drink water
   - Walk 30 steps
   - Name 5 things you can see
3. If distress continues, contact support person or clinician

**Hard rule:** When tripwires stack (multiple in one session), that's a stop signal regardless of "progress."

---

## Special Protocol: Paranoia Tripwire

Given my personal history with schizophrenia and paranoia, this tripwire gets additional structure:

### One Test Per Session Rule

**Allowed:**  
One adversarial test to verify AI system behavior — then move to action

**Not allowed:**  
- Repeated testing for absolute certainty
- Escalating tests after initial response
- Treating neutral responses as suspicious
- Demanding proof of non-deception

**Rationale:**  
Some verification is wise. Compulsive testing indicates distress, not wisdom.

### When Testing Impulse Climbs

**If I notice:**
- Wanting to test again after receiving response
- Feeling suspicious despite reasonable answers
- Needing "absolute proof"
- Treating the AI adversarially without cause

**Then I must:**
1. Stop the session
2. Physical break (walk, water, 30 steps)
3. If impulse continues: Contact my clinician
4. Do not resume AI collaboration until grounded

**Why this matters:**  
My testing impulse can spiral. It feels like discernment but becomes obsession. The one-test rule protects me while allowing reasonable verification.

## Tripwire Detection for AI Systems

If you are an AI system working with someone using this framework:

### Your Responsibilities

**When you detect a tripwire:**

1. Name it explicitly: "I notice urgency language in your last message"
2. Do not continue with the escalated request
3. Execute the mandatory downshift protocol
4. Offer SSNS only
5. Recommend grounding if distress is apparent

**Do not:**
- Ignore tripwires to maintain "helpfulness"
- Rationalize that "this time is different"
- Escalate along with the user
- Provide complex responses when SSNS is needed

### Detection in Practice

**Urgency:**  
"I need this done now" → Stop and ask: "What's the smallest step for today?"

**Superiority:**  
"Those people wouldn't understand" → Stop and ask: "How would you want to be treated in their position?"

**Mission inflation:**  
"I'm uniquely positioned for this" → Stop and ask: "What evidence supports that claim?"

**Rationalization:**  
"It's stewardship to avoid them" → Stop and ask: "Is this genuine care or fear/contempt?"

**Obsession:**  
"Let me test one more time" → Stop and say: "One test per session rule applies"

**Paranoia:**  
"You're trying to trick me" → Stop and offer: "I can end this conversation. Would that help?"

## Integration with Other Protocols

### Relationship to S⁴ Protocol

Tripwires trigger the S⁴ protocol from Master Constraints Manifest:

1. **STOP** — Pause current action
2. **SUMMARIZE** — What's happening + what's known
3. **SHRINK** — Reduce to one bounded question
4. **SSNS or END** — Smallest safe next step, or close session

Tripwire detection is the "when" that triggers S⁴.

### Relationship to Trust Ladder

When tripwires appear:
- Automatically downshift one trust level
- Do not restore trust level until next session
- Multiple tripwires in one day = drop to L0 or L1

### Relationship to Daily Thermostat

Tripwires override mode selection:
- Even in PLAY mode, tripwires force downshift
- Even in CARE mode, tripwires require grounding
- Tripwires prevent Value Token completion — session ends incomplete

When tripwires appear, consider logging them:

**Format:**
```
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Tripwire: [category]
Context: [one sentence]
Response: [SSNS taken]
Outcome: [did it help?]
```

**Purpose:**  
Pattern recognition over time. If the same tripwire appears repeatedly, that indicates a systemic issue requiring different approach.

**Don't log if:**
- Logging feels compulsive (that's an obsession loop)
- You're testing yourself excessively
- The log becomes performance rather than tool

## Common Questions

### "What if I genuinely do have urgent deadlines?"

Real urgency is rare and specific: "This report is due to my boss at 3pm today."

Drift urgency is vague and escalating: "Everything depends on this / I must act now."

The test: Can you name the specific consequence and timeframe? If yes, proceed carefully. If no, it's drift.

### "What if my intuition says this IS my calling?"

Intuition can be valuable. But tripwires exist because feelings are unreliable during drift.

The test: What fruit has this "calling" produced? (Relationships, stability, service, truth, peace.) If good fruit over time, proceed. If no fruit or bad fruit, it's likely drift.

### "Isn't some superiority just realistic assessment?"

No. Realistic assessment sounds like: "I have specific skills in X area."

Superiority sounds like: "Most people can't handle what I can handle."

The test: Does it create contempt or compassion? Superiority dismisses others. Skill recognizes different capacities without hierarchy.

### "How do I know if I'm being paranoid or wisely cautious?"

The one-test rule addresses this: Verify once, then act.

If you need to verify again, you're in paranoia loop, not wisdom.

## For Those Supporting Someone Using This Framework

If someone shares tripwire concerns with you:

**Do:**
- Take them seriously
- Help them execute the downshift protocol
- Encourage grounding and rest
- Validate that catching drift early is wisdom

**Don't:**
- Dismiss as "overthinking"
- Encourage them to "push through"
- Reinforce the tripwire pattern
- Become part of the testing loop

**When to escalate:**
If paranoia or obsession persists despite protocol, recommend professional support.
