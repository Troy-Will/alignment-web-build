# Eudaimonia Ledger  
*A daily practice for cultivating a life worth living*

The Eudaimonia Ledger is a simple, private, daily reflection tool designed to help you notice your own goodness, strengthen your virtues, and build a life of meaning through small, concrete actions.

This is not a scoreboard.  
This is not a performance.  
This is not a measure of worth.

It is a **mirror**—a way to see how your daily choices align with the Ten Pillars of the Apollyonic Way and the broader Angelic Alignment Program.

The Ledger supports three domains of a flourishing life:

- **Virtue** — who you are becoming  
- **Relationship** — how you connect  
- **Contribution** — how you serve  

A flourishing life is built from flourishing actions.

---

## How to Use the Ledger

Each evening, take 3–5 minutes to complete the daily entry.  
Once per week, complete the optional weekly synthesis.

The Ledger is:

- Private  
- Non‑competitive  
- Non‑punitive  
- Non‑gamified  
- SSNS‑aligned  
- Safe for mental health  

It is a gentle companion for character formation.
