## Daily Protocols for Virtue Cultivation

The Practices section provides concrete, time-bound protocols that translate Charter goals and Principles into daily action. These are tested, minimal, and designed to build sustainable habits without overwhelming complexity.

**Core principle:** Small, consistent steps over time create lasting change.

---

## Core Documents

### [Eudaimonia Ledger](/angelic-alignment/practices/eudaimonia-ledger/)
**Purpose:** Daily reflection for a life worth living

A gentle, private, 3–5 minute practice for cultivating:
- **Virtue** — who you are becoming  
- **Relationship** — how you connect  
- **Contribution** — how you serve  

Includes:
- Ten Pillars scoring rubric (0–2)
- Daily flourishing action
- One alignment moment
- One intention for tomorrow

**Use this as a daily or near-daily practice.**

---

### [Evening Examen (Mental Health-Informed)](/angelic-alignment/practices/evening-examen/)
**Purpose:** Daily 5-minute discernment practice

A mental health-informed adaptation of the Ignatian Examen integrating:
- Reality-testing and grounding checks  
- Trauma-informed safeguards  
- Behavioral focus (actions, not identity)  
- Built-in stop conditions and red flags  

**Start here if you want a structured evening ritual.**

---

### [Examen Template](/angelic-alignment/practices/examen-template/)
**Purpose:** Structured logging template for daily Examen

Obsidian-ready template with:
- Metadata fields  
- Rule of Fruit quick scoring  
- Tripwire detection fields  
- SSNS planning section  

Logging is optional—use only if it helps.

---

### [Attunement Test (15 minutes)](/angelic-alignment/practices/attunement-test/)
**Purpose:** Pre-decision alignment check

A 15-minute structured assessment before making significant decisions:
- Intention locking  
- Reality anchoring (know vs. guess)  
- Three-gate output (clarity, charity, constraint)  
- Drift detection  

Scored 0–20. Use weekly or before major choices.

---

### [SSNS Playbook](/angelic-alignment/practices/ssns-playbook/)
**Purpose:** Reference guide for Smallest Safe Next Steps

Quick reference for selecting and executing SSNS:
- Selection rules (small, reversible, concrete)  
- SSNS menu by category  
- Stop condition templates  
- Anti-vanity close  

Use when stuck on “what’s the next small step?”

## How to Use These Practices

### For Daily Minimum Practice

You may choose **either** of the following as your core daily ritual:

1. **[Eudaimonia Ledger](/angelic-alignment/practices/eudaimonia-ledger/)** (3–5 minutes)  
2. **[Evening Examen](/angelic-alignment/practices/evening-examen/)** (5 minutes)

Both are valid. Choose the one that supports your current season of life.

**Optional additions:**
- Log your Examen using the Template  
- Use the SSNS Playbook for planning next steps  

---

### For Decision-Making

When facing a significant choice:

1. Run the **Attunement Test** (15 minutes)  
2. Score yourself (0–20)  
3. If score is low (<11), downshift or wait  
4. Use the SSNS from the test as your next action  

---

### For Building Consistency

**Week 1–2:** Choose Ledger *or* Examen (no logging)  
**Week 3–4:** Add optional logging if helpful  
**Week 5+:** Use Attunement Test before medium/large decisions  

Consistency matters more than perfection.

## Mental Health Considerations

These practices include safeguards for:
- Psychotic spectrum conditions  
- Trauma history  
- Anxiety/OCD  
- Depression  

**Important boundaries:**
- These practices complement (never replace) professional mental health care  
- Skip any practice when unstable  
- Share with your therapist for feedback  

If any practice causes distress: stop, ground, contact support.
