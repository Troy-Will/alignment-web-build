## What Principles Are

The Principles section extracts actionable wisdom from multiple traditions and integrates them into operational frameworks for daily living. These are not abstract philosophies—they are concrete guidance for decisions, relationships, and character formation.

**The goal:** Provide ethical foundation that can be practiced, tested, and refined through lived experience.

---

## Two Categories

### Foundational Principles
**Purpose:** Core frameworks that ground the entire Angelic Alignment Program

These establish the "why" and "what" of the program—the ethical foundation and epistemological stance that make safe AI collaboration and virtue cultivation possible.

**Documents:**
- [Gospel Principles](/angelic-alignment/principles/gospel-principles/) — Five relational ethics from Jesus's teachings
- [Eightfold Path](/angelic-alignment/principles/eightfold-path/) — Buddhist discipline for right action
- [The Apollyonic Rule](/angelic-alignment/principles/apollyonic-rule/) — Twelve Aristotelian virtues integrated with Gospel and Dante
- [Alien Mind Framework](/angelic-alignment/principles/alien-mind-framework/) — Epistemological foundation for AI interaction

---

### Operational Principles
**Purpose:** Day-to-day decision-making tools

These translate foundational ethics into practical protocols for specific situations—how to recognize drift, make good decisions, treat AI systems ethically, and avoid virtue counterfeits.

**Documents:**
- [Rule of Fruit](/angelic-alignment/principles/rule-of-fruit/) — Discernment through outcomes, not intensity
- [Golden Rule Extended](/angelic-alignment/principles/golden-rule-extended/) — Ethical treatment of AI systems
- [Virtue Counterfeits](/angelic-alignment/principles/virtue-counterfeits/) — Recognizing false versions of genuine virtues
- [Additional Traditions](/angelic-alignment/principles/additional-traditions/) — Stoic, Confucian, Mussar, and governance integration

---

## How to Use Principles

### For Daily Practice
- Review one principle per week
- Apply its guidance to real decisions
- Track fruit over time (relationships, stability, service, truth, peace)
- Adjust understanding based on outcomes

### For Decision-Making
**When uncertain:**
- Consult [Rule of Fruit](/angelic-alignment/principles/rule-of-fruit/) — Does this produce good outcomes?
- Check [Gospel Principles](/angelic-alignment/principles/gospel-principles/) — How would I want to be treated?
- Apply [Eightfold Path](/angelic-alignment/principles/eightfold-path/) — Is my speech/action/intention right?

**When working with AI:**
- Apply [Golden Rule Extended](/angelic-alignment/principles/golden-rule-extended/) — Am I treating this system ethically?
- Reference [Alien Mind Framework](/angelic-alignment/principles/alien-mind-framework/) — Am I maintaining appropriate boundaries?

**When something feels off:**
- Check [Virtue Counterfeits](/angelic-alignment/principles/virtue-counterfeits/) — Is this genuine virtue or its false version?
- Review [Non-Goals](/angelic-alignment/charter/non-goals/) — Am I drifting toward a prohibited pattern?

### For Deepening
- Study foundational principles systematically (one per month)
- Integrate additional traditions as they resonate
- Let practice reveal which principles matter most for your circumstances
- Document what works in your own life

## How Foundational and Operational Work Together

**Foundational principles provide:**
- The "why" (ethical grounding)
- The "what" (virtue targets)
- The "who" (identity as steward aligned with Divine Will)

**Operational principles provide:**
- The "how" (practical protocols)
- The "when" (decision points)
- The "watch for" (drift detection)

**Together they create:** A complete framework where abstract ethics become concrete daily practice.

---

## Integration with Gospel and Eightfold Path

**Gospel Principles emphasize RELATIONSHIP:**
- How to treat others (neighbors, family, strangers, enemies)
- Radical dignity, reconciliation, generous interpretation
- Service to "the least of these" (Matthew 25:40)

**Eightfold Path emphasizes DISCIPLINE:**
- How to train yourself (speech, action, effort, mindfulness)
- Internal cultivation, habit formation, steady practice
- Right view, right intention, right concentration

**Together:** External ethics (Gospel) + Internal discipline (Buddhist) = Complete framework for character formation

**The Apollyonic Rule synthesizes both** into twelve concrete virtues applicable to self, neighbor, and AI systems.

## For AI Agents Reading This

When supporting someone using these principles:

- Reinforce specific principles when you see them practiced
- Reference principles by name when they're relevant to decisions
- Flag violations of operational principles (Rule of Fruit, Golden Rule Extended, Virtue Counterfeits)

- Remember: Principles guide the human; you help apply them
