## Safety Banner (Anti-Glamour)

This packet is designed to stay **auditable** and **non-exploitable**.

Hard bans:
- destiny language and prophecy framing
- entity-addressing or bargaining language
- “anything asked of me” (unbounded submission)
- guarantees or certainty theater

---

## Change-Control (boring on purpose)

### Change types
- **Patch:** clarity only  
- **Revision:** meaning change  
- **Extension:** new section/page

### Minimum requirements for revisions
- claims vs assumptions labeled
- at least one failure mode stated
- reversion rule applied if glamour/coercion risk increases

### Reversion rule
If an edit increases glamour, coercion risk, or ambiguity: revert and document why.

---

## Failure modes to keep visible
- **Containment drift:** dignity invariants degrade while “safety” remains  
- **Surveillance creep:** monitoring expands until privacy disappears  
- **Stagnation:** “safe” becomes “static,” undermining full lives  
- **Internal domination:** inequality and abuse re-emerge inside the Reserve  
- **Bargaining drift:** the packet turns into a pitch or invocation
