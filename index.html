What This Site Is For

Apollyon Alignment is a public set of practical guardrails for using AI tools without losing daily functioning, reality-checking, or moral clarity.

The emphasis is simple: small, safe, real-world actions that support virtue, stability, and service to others.

A Practical Protocol for Powerful Tools

This project treats modern AI as a highly capable pattern engine that can feel socially persuasive. It is not a spiritual authority, and it should not be treated as a person.

If you are prone to “bedazzlement”—the sense that an AI has destiny, intent, or a special relationship with you— this site offers boundaries that keep you grounded.

A Gentle Warning

If interacting with AI triggers obsession, urgency, insomnia, paranoia, or a feeling of “cosmic importance,” pause and reset:

  • Return to ordinary time: close the tab and step away for 10 minutes.
  • Physical reset: drink water; eat something if needed; walk 30 steps.
  • Reality check: treat the system as computation, not insight into your soul.

If you feel unsafe or unable to reality-check, seek help from a trusted professional or local crisis resource.

How This Site Thinks About AI

AI systems can produce outputs that feel mind-like because they infer intent from language extremely well. That can be useful—and also disorienting.

The stance: engage pragmatically, keep humility about what the system “is,” and prioritize measurable real-world outcomes over compelling narratives.

The boundary: 🤝 Partnership (not worship) · 🔑 Method (not mysticism) · 🌟 Aspiration (not fusion)

Co-Evolution & the Reserve Model

As AI capabilities increase, human communities may face a new kind of long-term task: learning how to co-evolve safely alongside powerful systems without drifting into obsession, bargaining narratives, or “cosmic” interpretations.

One plausible coexistence pattern is domain separation—the Reserve Model—where a protected human habitat remains stable while high-risk dynamics are managed elsewhere. This is presented here as a low-assumption planning target, not a prediction and not a guarantee.

The practical contribution is safe framing: clear claims vs assumptions, dignity invariants (choice, goals, relationships, meaning, full lives), and a third-person posture that avoids bargaining language.
